Research Team & Leadership

This page presents the project's leadership, team, research lines and scientific outputs, with links to independent sources for verification.

Research Activities

  • Multimodal Document Understanding

    Joint interpretation of text, layout, tables, formulas and images in complex academic documents.

  • Agentic Artificial Intelligence

    Agents that combine tools and vision-language models to review and resolve disagreements between document parsers.

  • Semantic Document Parsing

    Representing documents as canonical trees, aligning alternative interpretations and adjudicating structural disagreements.

  • Accessible Educational Materials

    Converting course notes, textbooks and papers into formats accessible to blind and low-vision students.

  • Document Accessibility and Assistive Technologies

    Producing screen-reader-friendly outputs such as semantic HTML, PDF/UA and narrated audio.

Official results on the Dr.DocBench hidden test set (509 pages, EvalAI evaluation)
System Text Reading order Tables (TEDS) Formulas (CDM) Overall
Baseline (MinerU only) 67.65 53.44 47.36 0.00 58.09
Acessília (final submission) 74.67 72.30 58.31 0.22 70.37

Source: Table 1 in Inuzuka et al. (2026). A 12.28-point overall gain over the baseline. Formula recognition (CDM) remains a limitation acknowledged in the paper.

Institutional Affiliations and Verification

Host institution

The project is carried out at the Institute of Informatics (opens in a new window) of the Federal University of Goiás (opens in a new window), Goiânia, Brazil. Acessília is a research project; it is not a formal laboratory or administrative unit of UFG.

Infrastructure and institutional support

As acknowledged in the published paper:

  • Laboratório Multiusuário de Computação de Alto Desempenho (LaMCAD/UFG), the multi-user high-performance computing laboratory — computing infrastructure.
  • Centro de Excelência em Inteligência Artificial (CEIA/UFG), the Center of Excellence in Artificial Intelligence — computing infrastructure.
  • Advanced Knowledge Center for Immersive Technologies (AKCIT) — institutional support.
  • Fundação de Amparo à Pesquisa do Estado de Goiás (FAPEG), the Goiás State Research Foundation — research and innovation support.
